Why ProblemHub Exists

"Real Problems. Meaningful Projects. Lasting Impact."

The Academic Project Dilemma

Every semester, thousands of talented engineering and computer science students spend months creating project reports, presentations, and code repositories. Unfortunately, an overwhelming majority end up building repetitive, trivial systems — online book stores, simple quiz applications, or basic hotel booking CRUD portals.

Meanwhile, local municipal corporations, grassroots non-profit charities (NGOs), government primary healthcare sub-centres, and regional agrarian cooperatives face genuine operational bottlenecks that simple, well-crafted software could completely solve. The gap is not a lack of student talent; it is the absence of a centralized bridge to discover authentic problems.

The ProblemHub Solution

ProblemHub is a centralized ecosystem where authentic challenges from industries, startups, NGOs, municipal councils, and innovation cells are crowdsourced, verified by faculty, and discovered by student teams based on their exact tech stack, difficulty, and semester requirements.

Crucially, ProblemHub also preserves completed student solutions. When a student batch finishes a project, their architecture, GitHub repository, and documented limitations become part of the platform's searchable knowledge base. The next batch of students can study their results and build direct extensions instead of reinventing the wheel from scratch.

Our Long-Term Vision

"Create a continuous ecosystem where every student project can become a foundation for the next generation."

By linking college coursework to real community impact, ProblemHub turns project evaluations from mere grading rituals into true engineering breakthroughs that help students gain industry-ready portfolios while delivering tangible benefits to society.
