Project Overview

About ProblemHub

An academic portal connecting students with real problem statements for semester projects.

Purpose of the Project

In academic curricula, students often face difficulty selecting meaningful problem statements for their mini and capstone projects. At the same time, community organizations, NGOs, and department research labs have genuine technical challenges that students can solve.

ProblemHub provides a centralized repository where real-world problem statements are published, claimed by student teams, mentored by faculty guides, and archived upon completion.

Platform Modules

Problem Repository

Organizations and faculty publish problem statements with required technologies, difficulty levels, and expected deliverables.

Project Proposals

Students browse challenges matching their skill tags and submit project proposals to initiate teamwork.

Milestone Tracking

Teams update progress from proposal (10%) to completion (100%), with faculty guide review and approval.

Solutions Library

Completed projects are preserved with code repositories, documentation, and future scope notes for future batches.
